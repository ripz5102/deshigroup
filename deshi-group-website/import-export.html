Business division

Restaurant Products Import and Export

Deshi Group works with manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors and food-service businesses to source suitable restaurant products and develop dependable supply relationships.

Product focus

Supporting restaurant and food-service requirements

We help explore practical product options and coordinate information across the supply process. Potential categories may include:

  • Commercial kitchen equipment
  • Kitchen utensils and accessories
  • Food packaging and disposable catering products
  • Tableware
  • Cleaning and hygiene products
  • Restaurant furniture
  • Operational supplies
  • Selected food-service products, subject to applicable regulations

Services
