Important safeguards

Transaction principles

Independent verification is essential. A commercial introduction is not a recommendation, guarantee or substitute for professional due diligence.
  • Parties must complete independent due diligence.
  • Commercial terms must be documented in contracts between the relevant parties.
  • Product, origin, quantity and specification documents must be independently verified.
  • Payment instruments must be verified through the parties’ own banks and professional advisers.
  • Transactions remain subject to sanctions, anti-money-laundering, export-control and other applicable requirements.
  • Deshi Group does not provide legal, financial or investment advice through this website.
  • Deshi Group does not guarantee the performance of buyers, sellers, banks, carriers or other third parties unless expressly agreed in a signed contract.

Transactions are subject to independent due diligence, contractual agreement, compliance checks and applicable laws.
